# Mission Reflection

The platform that impressed me the most is **Google Cloud** because of its strong AI and Machine Learning tools. I learned that Google Cloud can support companies that need high-performance computing and AI applications. I also learned that AWS and Microsoft Azure have their own strengths depending on the needs of the company.

The three cloud platforms have many similarities. AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud all offer compute, storage, database, and identity services. However, they have different approaches and strengths. AWS has many cloud services, Azure works well with Microsoft products, and Google Cloud is strong in AI, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes.

Business needs can greatly influence the choice of cloud platform. For example, I recommended AWS for the startup because it has many services that can support a growing application. For the university, I recommended Azure because it already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory. This showed me that the best platform depends on the client's situation and requirements.

As a consultant, I learned that the most popular platform is not always the best choice. We need to understand the client's budget, existing technology, business goals, and future plans before making a recommendation.

After completing this lab, my portfolio also improved. I learned more about Linux, cloud services, and comparing AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. I also added new documentation and organized my repository better. Overall, this lab helped me improve both my technical skills and my ability to make cloud recommendations based on real business needs.
